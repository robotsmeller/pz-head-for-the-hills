Doesn't it seem weird that you're home eating dinner and then all of a sudden there's just zombies everywhere? I don't know about you all, but if I even thought for a second zombies were coming to my hood, I'd be out the door and heading for the hills to ride it out before my town became infested.

Welcome to [b]Head for the Hills![/b]

You saw it coming and you left early. Pick "Remote Cabin" on the new game screen and you wake up in a cabin out in the woods with a truck outside and nobody for miles. Not a map mod, not an overhaul. Just somewhere else to start.

[b]Verified on Build 42.20 Stable.[/b]

Seventeen cabins, picked one at a time off the map. The game draws one at random when you start and does not tell you which. They run from a one-room shack to a farmhouse with seven rooms. Some sit a few hundred tiles from the nearest town and some are a couple of thousand, so which one you draw decides how bad your first week is.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]What you wake up with[/h2]

A vehicle parked outside, with the key on you.

The key to the cabin, because being locked out of your own hideout is a stupid way to start.

A well in the yard, if the place has no water of its own. Out in the grass where a well goes, not bolted to the back wall.

A generator against the house, if the place has none. It is not running. You can have it fuelled and wired in if you like, but you flip the switch yourself. Waking up to a generator already roaring in the middle of the woods rather defeats the point.

Whatever starting gear you picked, and the season you picked.

Anything the cabin already has, it keeps. No second well dug beside the first one.

[h2]All of it is a setting[/h2]

Every piece above can be turned off or changed on the sandbox page.

Two of the settings are proper pickers rather than text boxes. The starting vehicle is a dropdown of every vehicle currently loaded, vanilla and custom both. The starting equipment is a searchable list of every item currently loaded, same deal. They read your game as it is, so whatever your other mods add is already in them.

[h2]Somewhere specific in mind?[/h2]

Custom Start Coordinate takes a coordinate off [url=https://map.projectzomboid.com/]map.projectzomboid.com[/url] and starts you there instead. Paste it however the site gives it to you.

A spot you choose yourself gets none of the checking the seventeen had, so things may land oddly or not fit. If you pick a spot in the water it will move you to the nearest real ground, because nobody has ever wanted to start in a lake.

[h2]What it does NOT do[/h2]

No new map, no new tiles, no new items, no new traits.

It does not touch zombies, loot, weather or any mechanic. It puts you somewhere and hands you a few things. That is the whole mod.

It does not need any other mod, and everything it places is base game. If you have a mod that replaces wells, this one will notice and leave your cabin alone rather than putting somebody else's well in your yard.

It will not stop the woods being the woods. There is no safe zone. Whatever wandered up there before you did is still up there.

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]

Written for it and untested. World objects are created once by the server and inventory is handed out per player, which is how the base game does both. On a server settings screen the two pickers show as plain text boxes, so type a vehicle id like Base.PickUpTruck, and item ids separated by semicolons.

If you run it on a server, tell me how it went.
